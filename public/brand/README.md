The public logo and favicon are adapted from the supplied flat AW monogram. Navy is light and the blue swoosh is deep red on transparency. The glossy app-style logo is reserved for a future Admin/PWA asset and is not used here.


## WhatsApp contact icon

`whatsapp.svg` is the recognizable WhatsApp mark from Simple Icons, retrieved from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/whatsapp.svg. It retains the green #25D366 fill inside the portfolio's black/red contact button. The local SVG has no scripts or remote asset dependencies. It is decorative beside the accessible link label.
